# Reference Style Brief

> For human and high-fidelity agent use. This file explains how Poliform / Flexform style references should be translated into Liangqin Ji Mu language without copying their concrete form.

## Purpose

Use Poliform / Flexform as design-style references only. Extract their method, not their template.

## What To Borrow

- Image rhythm: let one strong image or material field carry the first impression.
- Whitespace: keep generous directional space around text, navigation and visual subjects.
- Text density: use fewer, calmer words; avoid stacked claims and feature callouts.
- Material focus: make wood, stone, fabric, leather, shadow and natural light do the emotional work.
- Brand confidence: navigation, CTA and labels should feel present but quiet.
- Editorial pacing: alternate wide breathing sections with precise information moments.

## What Not To Copy

- Do not copy a reference site's exact homepage structure.
- Do not copy furniture categories, exhibition scenes, campaign names, copywriting, logo behavior or interaction signatures.
- Do not force every output into a full-bleed homepage, showroom, product catalog or Italian furniture magazine layout.
- Do not replace Liangqin Ji Mu's Chinese readability and service trust with a cold European luxury tone.

## Translation Rule

First decide the requested carrier: web page, H5, quote card, product detail, case cover, poster, presentation page, or visual draft. Then apply the style method to that carrier.

## Review Questions

- Does the result feel quiet, tactile and ordered before it feels clickable?
- Is the main image or material field doing more work than cards, chips or icons?
- Can a Chinese customer understand the next step without being pushed?
- If the reference site disappeared, would this still feel like Liangqin Ji Mu?
