# Asset Needs

> Use this before high-fidelity visual generation. Missing assets should lower fidelity instead of being hidden behind fake polish.

## Minimum Useful Asset Set

- 5-8 horizontal space images for hero, case opening, or wide editorial sections.
- 6-10 vertical space images for mobile, long images, case covers, and social sharing.
- 10-20 material close-ups: wood grain, stone, fabric, leather, hardware, edges, joins, handles, lighting details.
- Product or case metadata: product name, material, room type, project context, available dimensions or price notes when relevant.
- Brand assets: readable horizontal logo, vertical logo if needed, and approved logo usage background.

## Quality Bar

- Images should be high resolution, naturally lit, low saturation, and not over-sharpened.
- Avoid cluttered phone snapshots, fake showroom renders, obvious stock-photo luxury, heavy filters, and inconsistent color temperature.
- A small set of real, coherent images is better than many mismatched decorative images.

## Fidelity Rules

- If no real imagery is available, output a structured spec or low-detail draft, not a final-looking premium visual.
- If logo files are missing or unreadable, do not produce final branded visuals.
- If product facts are missing, use placeholders and state the gap; do not invent dimensions, prices, case names or material claims.
